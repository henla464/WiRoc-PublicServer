Delete your account and data

This page explains how users of the WiRoc Config app (Android) and the related WiRoc Monitor website at monitor.wiroc.se, developed by WiRoc, can request that their account and associated data are deleted.

How to request deletion of your account

Deletion is self-service, is confirmed by email and takes effect immediately. Follow these steps:

  1. Go to monitor.wiroc.se and log in with the email address and password of the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the Delete account page: it is linked in the menu at the top right, or you can go directly to https://monitor.wiroc.se/deleteaccount.html.
  3. Click Send confirmation email.
  4. Open the confirmation email we send to your address and click the link in it (the link is valid for 30 minutes).
  5. On the page that opens, tick the confirmation box and click Delete my account.

Your account and the associated data listed below are then deleted immediately and cannot be restored.

If you cannot log in

If you can no longer access your account (for example, you have forgotten your password), send an email from the email address of the account to info@wiroc.se and ask us to delete it. We will verify that you own the account and process the request.

Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete the following personal data:

  • Your account credentials: email address and hashed password.
  • Password-recovery and account-deletion confirmation tokens.
  • Account metadata: account creation and update timestamps.
  • Device access grants linked to your account (which devices you had been given access to).
  • Competition access grants linked to your account (which competitions you had been given access to).

Data that is kept, and why

Some data is not deleted, because it belongs to shared competitions or to WiRoc devices rather than to your account alone. Keeping it avoids disrupting other users who share the same competition or device.

  • Competitions, controls and uploaded map files that you created. These remain part of the shared competition data, but are no longer linked to your personal account.
  • The numeric identifier of the user that created a competition or control, or that last set a device's competition. This identifier is kept but no longer resolves to a personal account once the account is deleted.
  • Device data and log archives uploaded by WiRoc devices. These are generated by the devices and are not personal account data.

Retention period

  • The personal data listed under Data that is deleted is removed immediately when you confirm the deletion. We do not keep it afterwards.
  • Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten.
